    KEY TERMS

    Sandbox
    A complete working prototype with fictional records. No real money moves, no live wallets, no cannabis transaction.
    Green Gate
    The qualified-partner check. A specifically qualified fixture partner (within this sandbox) must authorize each payment before it can proceed to the customer handoff. No real-world licensure or approval is implied.
    Watchers
    Two independent observers that each read the same on-chain evidence and must agree on slot, signature, amount, and destination before verification passes.
    Reconciliation
    Four-way comparison of POS record, chain evidence, partner amount, and the operational ledger. All four must agree before the run closes.
