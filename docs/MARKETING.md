# Green Light Pay Consumer marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance coordinates public materials. The GLP project owner must confirm any commercial agreement.

**Audience.** Merchant product teams evaluating a consumer payment flow. **Offer.** Let a shopper try a keypad, send to a handle and buy a sample item in one flow.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Show the same sample basket in the keypad, receipt and activity history. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Complete the sample basket. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 5 merchant walkthrough completions. |

### Sales test

Interview five operators about failed or ambiguous receipts. Offer one separately contracted integration assessment only when a buyer requests a scope. Qualify two design-partner discussions for paid integration work under a separate GLP agreement.

### Measurement and decision

Use `green-light-pay-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** Production funding, provider approval, custody, conversion and settlement remain unverified. GLP has separate finances and governance. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
